Top 10 Rappers
in India.

An editorial retrospective from GULLYGANG exploring the ten artists who have defined the sound, lyricism, street culture, and commercial evolution of Desi Hip-Hop.

8 Min Read
•
Curated by GULLYGANG Editorial
•
Updated September 2026

Indian hip-hop has transformed from an underground cypher subculture into one of the most vibrant, multi-lingual musical movements in the world. From the gullies of Mumbai and the battle circuits of New Delhi to Bengaluru’s classical-fusion rhyme books and Pune’s 808 trap landscapes, Indian rap reflects the raw realities, ambitions, and linguistic diversity of modern South Asia.

Important editorial note: Musical artistry is inherently personal and subjective. This guide is an editorial ranking curated by the GULLYGANG team to honor artists whose catalogs, technical penmanship, and cultural footprints have meaningfully pushed the genre forward. It is not an objective universal verdict, but a comprehensive exploration for music lovers seeking the foundational voices of Indian hip-hop.

How We Ranked Them

Our editorial team evaluated artists across five core pillars to balance underground authenticity with broader cultural impact.

01

Cultural Influence

Pioneering moments, building platforms, and opening doors for regional subcultures.

02

Body of Work

Album consistency, thematic vision, iconic singles, and enduring replay value over years.

03

Lyrical Craft

Rhyme scheme density, multisyllabic complexity, double entendres, and storytelling depth.

04

Originality & Flow

Distinct vocal cadence, authentic vernacular, unique rhythm pockets, and sonic individuality.

05

Audience Resonance

Organic community devotion, electric live performance energy, and lasting cultural loyalty.

01

DIVINE

Vivian Fernandes • Mumbai
Gully Rap Pioneer Gully Gang Founder Street Realism

DIVINE stands as the transformative spearhead who took Hindi street rap from the gullies of JB Nagar in Andheri East and thrust it into the global music consciousness. His breakout singles Yeh Mera Bombay (2013) and Mere Gully Mein (2015) created the blueprint for Indian street hip-hop, proving that authentic vernacular narratives could capture millions without catering to conventional commercial compromises.

Why He’s Included: Beyond his prolific album discography—including landmark projects like Kohinoor (2019), Punya Paap (2020), and Gunehgar (2022)—DIVINE created Gully Gang Entertainment to discover, manage, and empower independent Indian talent. His international collaborations with icons like Nas and Dave East cemented India’s presence on the global hip-hop map.

Notable Style & Strength

Commanding baritone delivery, unyielding conviction, and raw socio-economic street commentary woven into unforgettable anthemic hooks.

Essential Tracks

Mere Gully Mein (feat. Naezy) Kohinoor Kaam 25 3:59 AM
02

KR$NA

Krishna Kaul • New Delhi
Technical Penmanship Multisyllabic Rhymes Kalamkaar

KR$NA (formerly Young Prozpekt) is widely celebrated as the pinnacle of lyrical complexity in Indian hip-hop. Having honed his craft through the early underground cypher era in English, his transition into Hindi lyricism with tracks like Vyanjan (2016)—a masterclass where every line began with consecutive Hindi consonants—rewrote the standards of technical rhyming in South Asia.

Why He’s Included: KR$NA proved that intricate penmanship, layered double entendres, and complex rhyme structures could build an intensely passionate mainstream following without dumbing down content. Projects like Still Here (2021) and Far From Over (2023) established him as one of the most formidable battle and track lyricists on the continent.

Notable Style & Strength

Dense multisyllabic rhyme schemes, razor-sharp punchlines, clinical breath control, and multi-layered wordplay that rewards repeated listening.

Essential Tracks

Vyanjan Makasam Say My Name Prarthana
03

Raftaar

Dilin Nair • New Delhi / Kerala
Pioneer & Producer Kalamkaar Co-founder Chopper Flow

Raftaar has served as a foundational cornerstone of modern Indian hip-hop for over a decade. Starting with the seminal Mafia Mundeer collective and breaking out with explosive anthems like Swag Mera Desi (2014), Raftaar pioneered rapid-fire chopper flows in Hindi while mastering full-scale music production and executive mentorship.

Why He’s Included: Beyond his undeniable flow versatility across boom-bap, drill, trap, and experimental sounds (exemplified in his acclaimed Hard Drive Vol. 1 EP), Raftaar co-founded the Kalamkaar label to provide infrastructure, distribution, and mentorship to rising independent artists across the country.

Notable Style & Strength

Explosive cadence transitions, elite breath stamina, multi-genre music production, and thought-provoking socio-political anthems like Mantoiyat.

Essential Tracks

Swag Mera Desi Mantoiyat Dilli Waali Baatcheet No China
04

Seedhe Maut

Encore ABJ & Calm • New Delhi
Dynamic Duo Azadi Records New Wave DHH

The New Delhi duo of Siddhant Sharma (Encore ABJ) and Abhijay Negi (Calm), backed by visionary producer Sez on the Beat, redefined the soundscape of contemporary Indian hip-hop. Their landmark debut Bayaan (2019) followed by the modern masterpiece Nayaab (2022) captured the raw anxieties, humor, ambitions, and brotherhood of Delhi youth culture with unprecedented intimacy.

Why They’re Included: Seedhe Maut possess arguably the most seamless vocal chemistry in Indian music. With extensive projects like Lunch Break (2023) and Kshma, they proved that uncompromising independent album craftsmanship can build a die-hard nationwide subculture ("TBSM").

Notable Style & Strength

Interlocking back-to-back delivery, Calm’s polyrhythmic flow pockets, Encore ABJ’s poignant philosophical lyricism, and electrifying live energy.

Essential Tracks

101 Namastute Nanchaku (feat. MC Stan) Keh Chuka
05

Emiway Bantai

Bilal Shaikh • Mumbai
Independent Phenom Bantai Records Grassroots Viral Anthems

Hailing from Antop Hill, Mumbai, Emiway Bantai is one of the most prolific independent success stories in global music. Rising through YouTube with zero corporate backing, his explosive colloquial slang, high-energy delivery, and infectious hooks—epitomized by the historic viral franchise Machayenge—amassed billions of streams worldwide.

Why He’s Included: Emiway proved that independent artists in India could rival major record labels on their own terms. Through Bantai Records, extensive album drops like Malum Hai Na and King of the Streets, and collaborations with international stars like Snoop Dogg and Dax, he remains an unyielding force of independent desi hip-hop.

Notable Style & Strength

Unfiltered Bombay street slang, instant hook memorability, relentless output velocity, and dynamic tempo switches from laid-back banter to rapid-fire delivery.

Essential Tracks

Machayenge Aur Bantai Giraftaar Khatam Hue Waande
06

Naezy

Naved Shaikh • Kurla, Mumbai
Gully Rap Originator Kurla Street Voice Urdu Cadence

In January 2014, a DIY zero-budget music video shot on an iPad in Kurla titled Aafat! exploded across the internet. That artist was Naezy the Baa. With an unprecedented cadence blending complex Urdu vocabulary with authentic Mumbai street colloquialisms, Naezy directly sparked the modern Indian street rap movement.

Why He’s Included: Alongside DIVINE, Naezy co-created the historic anthem Mere Gully Mein and served as the real-life inspiration behind Zoya Akhtar’s acclaimed film Gully Boy (2019). His organic storytelling about civic realities, corruption, and working-class ambition laid the moral foundation of desi hip-hop.

Notable Style & Strength

Unique off-beat flow pockets, rich colloquial Urdu rhyming, organic humor, and hard-hitting socially conscious street journalism.

Essential Tracks

Aafat! Mere Gully Mein Haq Hai Asal Hustle
07

Prabh Deep

Prabhdeep Singh • Tilak Nagar, New Delhi
Conceptual Mastermind Punjabi Introspection Jazz-Hip-Hop Fusion

Hailing from Tilak Nagar, West Delhi, Prabh Deep is widely regarded as one of Indian music’s most profound conceptual visionaries. His seminal 2017 debut Class-Sikh was a gripping narrative exploring drug crises, youth disorientation, and generational trauma. He followed it with Tabia (2021), a philosophical jazz-hop opus, and King (2019/2023).

Why He’s Included: Prabh Deep expanded the sonic boundaries of Indian hip-hop beyond straightforward boom-bap, incorporating live brass, jazz arrangements, and introspective vocal harmonies while delivering poetic Punjabi verses that tackle existential redemption.

Notable Style & Strength

Cinematic concept album world-building, raw emotional vulnerability, atmospheric jazz-hop musicality, and deeply introspective Punjabi storytelling.

Essential Tracks

Suno Chitta Amar Paapi
08

MC Stan

Altaf Shaikh • Pune
New Wave Trap Pioneer Self-Produced 808s Subcultural Icon

Emerging from the Tadiwala Road neighborhood in Pune, MC Stan became one of the most polarizing yet influential sonic pioneers of Indian new-wave rap. A self-taught music producer who creates his own haunting 808s and hypnotic trap beats, Stan pioneered a distinct vernacular mixing Hindi, Marathi, and street slang that captured millions of Gen-Z listeners.

Why He’s Included: His autobiographical concept album Tadipaar (2020) is widely considered one of the finest produced storytelling projects in Indian hip-hop history. His raw vulnerability on tracks like Astagfirullah and immense youth subculture influence established him as a true trendsetter.

Notable Style & Strength

Hypnotic self-produced trap arrangements, raw confessional lyricism, distinctive mumble/sing-rap pockets, and trendsetting street fashion aesthetics.

Essential Tracks

Wata Astagfirullah Tadipaar Basti Ka Hasti
09

Brodha V

Vighnesh Shivanand • Bengaluru
Classical Fusion English Penmanship South Indian Pioneer

Representing Bengaluru, Brodha V is one of the earliest pioneers of Indian underground hip-hop, having emerged in the late 2000s with the Machas With Attitude crew. He pioneered a groundbreaking signature sound: blending Carnatic and Hindustani classical ragas with breathless, multisyllabic English hip-hop verses.

Why He’s Included: Landmark singles like Aathma Raama (2012) and Aigiri Nandini (2014) introduced an entirely original fusion template. His pristine breath control, complex rhyme structures, and uplifting philosophical storytelling have earned him universal respect across generations of artists.

Notable Style & Strength

Flawless fast-flow breath stamina, intricate English multisyllabic rhyme schemes, traditional Sanskrit chant integration, and uplifting philosophical themes.

Essential Tracks

Aathma Raama On My Own Aigiri Nandini Vainko
10

King

Arpan Kumar Chandel • New Delhi
Melodic Rap Pioneer Global Crossover Anthemic Songwriting

Rising from New Delhi through MTV Hustle Season 1, King became the definitive melodic crossover icon of modern Indian hip-hop. Demonstrating an unmatched instinct for unforgettable hooks, emotional romantic lyricism, and modern pop-rap production, King took Indian urban music to global viral charts.

Why He’s Included: Hits like Tu Aake Dekhle and the record-breaking Maan Meri Jaan (later remixed with international pop star Nick Jonas) proved the massive global commercial viability of Hindi melodic rap. Albums like The Carnival and Champagne Talk bridge street rap roots with timeless mainstream pop sensibilities.

Notable Style & Strength

Soulful melodic hook craftsmanship, emotive storytelling, vocal warmth, and effortless genre blending across contemporary R&B, pop, and rap.

Essential Tracks

Tu Aake Dekhle Maan Meri Jaan Pablo IICONIC

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is considered the pioneer of Gully Rap in India?

DIVINE and Naezy are widely recognized as the pioneers who ignited the Gully Rap movement from the streets of Mumbai, immortalized by their historic 2015 joint single Mere Gully Mein and the cinematic inspiration behind Gully Boy.

What defines great penmanship in Indian hip-hop?

Great penmanship in Desi Hip-Hop encompasses complex multisyllabic rhyming, cultural and regional authenticity, double/triple entendres, cohesive album storytelling, and the ability to articulate social realities with poetic precision.

How frequently is this GULLYGANG editorial list updated?

The Indian hip-hop ecosystem is continuously evolving with new albums, breakout talents, and stylistic shifts. GULLYGANG’s editorial board periodically reviews discographies and cultural impact to update this guide.
